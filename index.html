Negotiation

Use a calm, evidence-based discussion about role scope, total compensation and competing data.

Conversation script

“I'm excited about the role. Based on the scope, level and overall compensation structure, I'd like to discuss whether there is room to move the package toward my target range. I'm happy to share relevant offer context and the impact I can bring.”

Checklist

1. Separate base, bonus and equity.
2. Compare equity vesting and annualization.
3. Confirm refreshers and bonus targets.
4. Ask about sign-on or one-time components.
5. Get the final structure in writing.
