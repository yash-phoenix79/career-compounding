# Tech Role Models

People whose work I want to study as I grow into a stronger software engineer and builder. This is an inspiration list, not an expectation to copy their careers or personalities.

## People to follow and learn from

- **[Hassan El Mghari (@nutlope)](https://x.com/nutlope)** — Study open-source AI apps, rapid product experiments, developer experience, design quality, and how small projects are shipped and shared.
- **[Tanay Kothari](https://www.google.com/search?q=Tanay+Kothari+software+engineer)** — Study his technical work, building approach, and career decisions. Add primary links to specific projects or talks as I identify the ones most relevant to me.
- **[Lauren Tan](https://www.google.com/search?q=Lauren+Tan+software+engineer)** — Study her engineering work, technical communication, and approach to building.
- **[Harkirat Singh](https://www.google.com/search?q=Harkirat+Singh+software+engineer)** — Study his teaching, practical project-building, and ability to help developers learn by doing.
- **[Khushi Panwar (@smilewithkhushi)](https://x.com/smilewithkhushi)** — Study her blend of developer advocacy, technical education, content creation, and community/ecosystem building across Web3, AI, and developer tools. Focus on how she makes complex technology approachable and helps developers discover and adopt products. Treat public profile claims as self-reported unless independently verified.

## What to learn from them

Focus on repeatable practices rather than status or online activity:

- Build and ship real projects.
- Learn by reading code and reproducing useful ideas.
- Develop strong product taste and care about the user experience.
- Explain technical ideas clearly.
- Share work, get feedback, and iterate.
- Build fundamentals alongside speed.
- Keep a record of lessons that translate into my own work.

## Monthly review

Once a month, choose **one concrete thing** to study from one person:
- A repository, technical talk, article, or product.
- The specific engineering or product lesson.
- One small experiment to apply that lesson.
- What I learned after trying it.

The goal is to become a capable engineer with my own judgment, not to imitate someone else's identity.
